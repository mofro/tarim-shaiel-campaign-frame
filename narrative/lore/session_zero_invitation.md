# The Wanderers Convene

*The call has gone out. The world remembers you — even if you don't remember it yet.*

Before the adventure begins, let us gather to build the characters who will walk the Silk Road. Session Zero is where origin stories take shape, where the party finds its footing, and where the world of Tarim Shaiel comes alive around the table.

No prior experience with Daggerheart or Tarim Shaiel is needed. Come curious.
