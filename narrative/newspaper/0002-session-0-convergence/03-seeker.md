The Seeker woke on a rocky outcrop above the Glass Dunes at dusk, a worn tome in their hands they didn't remember picking up. The pages were dense with star charts — someone's absolute certainty about the sky.

The stars and the charts didn't match. Not dramatically. The kind of wrong that could be fever or could be fact.

The tome was clear: northeast, where an oasis waited with water and survival. The logic was airtight. The calculation old but sound. Northeast was the correct answer for someone who wanted to live.

Their body said west. No chart. No proof. Something that didn't have words.

They went west.

The tome pushed back — not in words but in certainty arriving fully formed, the specific confidence of a thing that knows it's right. Every step west was a step away from survival toward nothing. They kept walking.

By midday the heat had become a negotiation between will and body. By afternoon they stopped negotiating. By dusk their legs forgot how to coordinate.

They fell. Didn't get up.

Outriders from Volkath's caravan, moving parallel to the dunes on the western road, almost rode past them — one more shape the desert had finished with. Someone reined in. Digging them free took longer than expected: whatever moved under that skin wasn't flesh giving way normally. Sand packed into every seam.

They woke in the back of a wagon, the tome still locked in their grip, stars sliding past the canvas overhead.

*Back with us?* A woman's voice, unsure what answer to expect.

They didn't have one yet.
