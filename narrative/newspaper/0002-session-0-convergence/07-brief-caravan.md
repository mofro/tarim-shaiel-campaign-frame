headline: "Volkath's Caravan Forms, Moves West"

A merchant caravan under Volkath — Tulpar, trading under Sixth Cycle papers — departed Rabati Malik bound for points further west. Survivors from the eastern roads have joined it in growing numbers. Three days to the next waystation, more after that. It moves at dawn and does not stop for weather.
