# Ariel — Seraph Patron Design History

*Design session: 2026-08-12. Three options developed; Marc chose Option C. Canonical file: [[narrative/gm_secrets/divine-players/the-hunting-hour]].*

---

## Options Considered

### Option A — The Unturning Eye
Deity of absolute witness. Not justice — **seeing without exception**. Champions sent to witness accurately, because what a divine witness sees becomes real in ways that cannot be undone. Cold, heavy, ancient. Presence is not comfort but the stillness of something that cannot stop. The patron's ask: Ariel cannot look away — the compulsion is literal.

Resonance: strongest match to the Surrendered Layer (Perceptual — "the right to look away") and the Vaghri theology of *being seen* as the highest relational gift. Intellectually darkest option; heaviest as a player experience.

### Option B — The Eclipsed
Once the great wing-lord of a prior order; eclipsed in some cosmological event, not destroyed but covered. No institutional backing, no temple. Ariel acts with the certainty of a god the world has decided to ignore. Something personal at stake — the eclipse was done *to them*, by forces still present in the cosmological landscape.

Resonance: strongest long-term arc potential; feeds directly into "was she really chosen, or just convenient?" as Ariel's central arc question. Best player-engagement hook through mystery and withheld history.

### Option C — The Hunting Hour ✓ *chosen*
Night-sky raptor deity: the predator that hunts when all other hunters have gone blind. Domain is sight in darkness — literal and broader. Natural patron-people are Vaghri. The Ethereal Visage ("supernatural visage strikes awe and fear") is the patron's direct expression, not Ariel's personal charisma. Theology: everything is prey or hunter in any given moment, and the difference is clarity.

Resonance: most immediately playable; the dark is baked into the mechanics every session. Strongest aesthetic match to Marc's character concept.

---

## Recommendation That Was Given

Option C as the primary pitch, Option B as the immediate alternative.

**Hybrid synthesis (adopted into canonical file):** The Hunting Hour was eclipsed. The patron was once fully manifest — the great night-raptor in clear cosmic sight — and was covered by deliberate intervention. They have been working through champions ever since. This grafts Option C's aesthetic and mechanical groundedness onto Option B's hidden-history arc. The darkness of Ariel's wings isn't temperament; it's the mark of a deity who has been in eclipse so long that the dark became entirely theirs.
