The mud receives without judgment. This I recorded at the waystation of Qal'at Umm, where the southern runoff had taken three merchants and returned two of them changed in ways neither could name. What the earth holds, it holds completely — weight, warmth, the particular shape of what was pressed into it. What it releases, it releases imperfectly. Something of the original arrangement remains in the clay. Something of the clay remains in what was held.

I do not know what the earth asks in exchange. I only know it asks.

— Attributed to Yūsra al-Mahdi, itinerant chronicler / recovered from a caravanserai wall, 1158 HB
