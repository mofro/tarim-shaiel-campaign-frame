# Session 4 — Prep: Qarshi

⚠️ **GM-ONLY.** A plan for the arrival in [[qarshi|Qarshi]] and the first day and night there. Every scene is modular: run them in any order, drop any of them.

**Sources.** The Session 3 player summary and the [[Session_3_Prep|Session 3 prep]]. The Qarshi town data comes from `world/maps/cities/derived/Qarshi.gm.json` (FTG seed 1, profile `world/maps/cities/profiles/Qarshi.yaml`). Stat lines come from the town inspector's Daggerheart pass (`utilities/geojson/town_daggerheart.py`, #409). The faction stubs are in `world/factions/`.

**Marking.** Material tagged **[INVENTED]** is new tonight and is not in any file. `qarshi.md` is still a placeholder, so everything about the town's character is [INVENTED] except names, buildings, jobs, hours and faction seats, which come from the generator. **[UNVERIFIED]** marks a detail not checked against canon.

## 1. Session Frame

**Where we left off.** The party slept in the militia town below the pass and left for Qarshi at dawn. Three things carry over:

- Cal read the town's laws, and the militia watched him do it.
- Rill's coin twitched during the tavern celebration.
- All the companions share a sense that something about that town was not right.

**The shape of tonight.** The party arrives, gets through the gate, finds lodging, and has a night in the city and a market morning. Three threads pull them onward:

| Thread | What it offers |
|---|---|
| The Chain-Breakers | Folk memory of the heroes' return |
| The temples | A lead toward Nādir and the Azure Convocation |
| The mercenaries | Paid work on the road south |

**Calendar [INVENTED, adjust freely].** They arrive **Wednesday afternoon**. In the data, the Chieftain Market (1062) opens **Thursdays only, 06:00–15:00**, and shops close on **Friday**. Arriving the day before market day gives the town a clock: it is full of traders tonight, the market is busy tomorrow, and the shutters come down the day after.

**Target pacing (~3 hrs).**

| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| Road in | 15 min |
| Gate | 20 min |
| Lodging and settling in | 20 min |
| Evening split: Pork and Crow / temples / Keep | 75 min |
| Market morning | 30 min |
| Hooks out | 15 min |

## 2. The Road In (optional, 10–15 min)

The Session 3 prep has unused road events. Take one, or skip straight to the walls.

- **Levy checkpoint [INVENTED].** A Watch post stands two hours out of the city. The Watch knows the caravan is coming, and maybe the militia town sent word. This is the price of Cal's legal enquiries: the town wrote him down. *"A stone man who asks for the laws"* has gone ahead of them.
- **Traffic.** Grain carts head for Thursday's market. A Tulpar family is moving its herd in. A Rahban tinker sits under an awning on a cart and calls to Sahir in the trade cant **[INVENTED]**.

**Sensory anchor for the first sight of the city.** Grey-brown walls rise out of irrigated green. Two minarets stand at different heights: the Lamp and the Fire (§6). The Keep sits low and broad in the middle. In the late afternoon the smoke of cook-fires already hangs flat over the caravan quarter.

## 3. The Gate — Oxus Watch Order (barracks 256)

Barracks 256 is an [[oxus-watch-order|Oxus Watch Order]] seat. Its staff are mostly Kuhban, which makes it a good gate post **[INVENTED placement]**. The Watch holds the street and defers to the Tulpar Confederation in matters of war.

**On the gate:**

- **Bilge Hearth-Stone** (Kuhban, 113, m). The sergeant. Old and patient, and impossible to hurry. He has seen every kind of traveller and is impressed by none of them.
- **Laleh Seam-Reader the Left-Handed** (Kuhban, 82, f). She reads papers and cargo. Her byname is literal: she checks the seams of bales for hidden goods **[INVENTED]**.
- Also on the roster: Soraya Anvil-Sworn the Quiet, and Bagavand Third-Gate (99).

**The scene.**

1. **The tariff.** Dilshod **"Second Seal"** earns his name. He handles the seven-day tariff papers, and this is his scene to shine or to stumble. One roll (Presence or Knowledge, Difficulty 12) either gets the papers stamped cheaply or brings a cargo inspection.
2. **The word from the militia [INVENTED].** If the levy checkpoint ran, Bilge has heard of the stone man. He asks Cal, deadpan, whether he would *like to see the law*. Then he points to a board of posted ordinances by the gate. There are seven, and the one about blades in the market is circled in charcoal.
3. **Ancestry looks.** The town data has no Simiah and no Faun at all. There are 45 Vaghri and 29 Tadbir in a town of about 6,300. Rill and Reizan will draw stares. Cal will draw less than he expects: a Tadbir ward-keeper is not unheard of here **[INVENTED]**. Ariel's wings get a note written on Laleh's tally.

**If it goes wrong.** The gate is not a fight; it is a fine. If it becomes one, it ends quickly: the Watch whistles, and a second barracks (257) is three streets away.

## 4. Lodging

The data has four caravanserais. Two are prepped.

### Default: The Cobbler's Lover (8811)

- **Warden Karghul Mansurzoda** (Tulpar, 86, f, lapsed). A veteran with a scarred jaw who keeps a short sword on the counter. The crew, Kettle and Forty, are Tulpar, and she gives them the good stalls on sight **[INVENTED]**.
- **Yarkan Otabekzoda the Tall**, the stable-hand. The Bizhanzoda family works the kitchen and rooms.

**The Volkath and Temir thread [INVENTED].** Karghul served alongside men who knew Volkath. If the talk turns to the road or the mountains, she knows the name and spits. She has heard that *the boy who set Volkath's prisoners loose* is gathering people under a chain sign somewhere north. That boy is Temir. This plants the Chain-follower group from the Session 3 prep without anyone needing to meet Temir yet. **Hold Volkath's remorseful tale** for an actual meeting, as Session 3 planned.

### Alternative: The Stylish Boar (12452), for Sahir

- **Warden Tannaz Faramarzugli** (29). Young, and newly in charge.
- **The Low-Gate family.** Homa (45) and Zafar (70) are Rahban, and so is **Mehrnoz Iskanderian**, the cook (Rahban, 68). **Mehran Bijanugli "Half-Price"** is the porter and haggles over everything.

**Hook [INVENTED].** Zafar Low-Gate recognises the iron compass's maker's mark, or claims to. Either it ties back to Kalus or Zafar is guessing. That is your call, and it pairs with the market merchant in §7.

The other two caravanserais are unprepped. The Grain and Throne (583) has the cook Nodir Bakhtiyarzadeh the Tall. The Monastery and Warhorse (12425) has a warden, Nasrin Kimak, who is only 19.

**Bathhouses.** The town has eight, e.g. the Alchiian (422) and the Nostalgic (476). After the pass and the wolves, a bathhouse is a cheap, warm scene for anyone who skips the evening's main thread. It is also a good place to overhear things (§8).

## 5. Night — The Pork and Crow (4), Chain-Breakers seat

**Open 20:00–03:00.** In the data this is a gang hangout. The profile makes it the [[chain-breakers-order|Chain-Breakers Order]] seat, following the Dehdadi precedent.

**What the Chain-Breakers are (from the faction file).** They hold the memory of the liberation and the chain-breaking rite. They have no headquarters and are anchored among the Tulpar. They believe the liberation was left incomplete and that **the heroes will return**. They hold memory, not understanding. The Scholar's Purge of ~1175 hunted them, so they keep their rites behind a tavern's back room.

**Who is here:**

| Name | Notes | Stat line |
|---|---|---|
| **Sepideh Zarirzoda the Younger** | Human, 53, f, Forge-Record faith. The boss. Thoughtful, slow to trust, and polite in the way that means she has already counted your knives. | Leader |
| **Ruksana Almaszoda** | 82, non-religious. **Keeper of the rite [INVENTED role].** She knows the words without believing in any god behind them. | Social-grade; the data lists her as a gang member, use Bruiser if it matters |
| **Alp Mehranzoda the Tall** | 52, the door | Bruiser |
| Ilkhan Hamidzoda, Hamid Samanzadeh | Hands | Bruiser |
| **Umida Shapurugli** | 19, the youngest member. Eager, and the one most likely to talk too much. | Bruiser |
| Tegin Jahanzadeh | 59 | — |
| **Aybek Slag-Hand**, **Golnar Tally-Hammer** (66) | Kuhban bartenders | Minion |
| Ramesh Dilshodugli, Berke Aybekian (79), Yazdan Ravshanzoda | Servers | — |
| Farid and Zilola Qunghirat | Cooks | — |

**The scene [INVENTED].**

1. **The room.** It is low-ceilinged and loud, with pork fat and crow pie, which the house is named for. Iron links hang from the rafters like a merchant's string of onions. Anyone sharp sees that every link is **broken**.
2. **The rite at midnight.** The back room goes quiet. Ruksana takes a whole chain and lays it across a newcomer's wrists; tonight the newcomer is a Tulpar drover. She speaks the old formula, and a hammer comes down. The words are half-remembered, and people mouth them along the way people mouth a hymn they never learned properly. **Write the formula in fragments.** No one in the room understands it fully, and that is the point.
3. **The coin.** At the hammer-blow, **Rill's coin turns over in his pocket once**, and only once. It does not twitch the way it did in the militia town. It *turns*, as if to look. The Jade Illusionist is watching this. It reveals at structurally perfect moments, and this is not one yet. Nothing more happens.
4. **The folk story.** Afterwards a drunk man tells the house version: the heroes went up into the sky after breaking the chains, and *they'll come back when the chains are whole again, to break them twice.* Others correct him; everyone has a different version. **Do not let any NPC link the PCs to the heroes.** At most Umida says to Veyra, admiringly, "You fight like the old songs." That is a compliment and nothing more.
5. **The ask (optional).** Sepideh has noticed the party's caravan. Her people need something carried quietly south: a letter, or a child of a member who must leave the city. She asks once and accepts a no. This is a hook, not a quest.

**If Rill or Sahir pushes.** Ask about the chain-sign group in the north, and Sepideh's face closes. Temir's people are *not* hers. She calls them "the ones who think the rite is a weapon". This sets up the Chain-follower split from the Session 3 prep **[INVENTED framing]**.

## 6. The Temples (afternoon or morning)

Both mosques are open **05:00–21:00**.

### Mosque of Uren — the Lamp (3)

- **Priest Hilola Mansurzadeh** (77). Social, Tier 2: Difficulty 14, HP 5, Stress 4, thresholds 9 / 18.
- **Cleric Artavan Khazar the Younger.**

**The Nādir lead [INVENTED].** The Lamp keeps the old lending library, or what the Purge left of it. Hilola corresponds with an **Azure Convocation** scholar teaching near **Puli Khumri**. That scholar is Nādir, who is living, per the Session 3 prep. The Puli Khumri Azure outpost itself is [INVENTED] in that prep. Hilola will write a letter of introduction for anyone who shows respect and asks good questions. Cal asking about law codes counts, and so does Sahir asking about old songs.

*Her price:* she wants word of the burned city, Kitabdara, if anyone saw it. A library-keeper asks after every lost library.

### Mosque of Nuona — the Fire (14)

- **Priest Almas Sorushian the Quiet** (54). Social, Tier 2, same line as Hilola.
- **Cleric Zulfiya Varzakkian the Left-Handed** (69).

**The optional dead-zone beat [INVENTED].** Use it once, briefly, and do not explain it.

- **The flame.** The eternal flame gutters when the party enters, for one breath.
- **Cal's tome.** Cal's tome will not open; the clasp holds as if rusted, and then it works.
- **Reizan.** Reizan's shift takes two tries.
- **Rill.** Rill feels nothing at all, and feels *calm*. Note that for the GM.

Almas notices the flame, says nothing, and later asks the party to leave by the side door, *politely*. This is a practical effect only. The cosmology stays offstage.

## 7. Market Morning — Chieftain Market (1062), Thursday 06:00–15:00

This is a merchant-guilds seat. The [[merchant-guilds|merchant guilds]] file is mostly TODO, so any guild detail tonight is [INVENTED].

**The merchant with the jade abacus.** The Session 3 prep's unnamed merchant carried a **balsa compass case and a jade abacus**. In Session 1, **Kalus** carried a jade abacus and gave Sahir the iron compass. **Whether they are the same man is your call.** Three ways to run it:

- **(a) Kalus himself.** He is surprised and pleased to see Sahir, and asks whether the compass "still points where it should". This is the warm option and confirms a recurring friend.
- **(b) A guild colleague of Kalus's.** The jade abacus is a guild token. He has heard of Sahir and is wary. This opens up the merchant guilds.
- **(c) A coincidence.** The jade abacus is fashionable this year. That deflates the thread, but cheaply.

**Market colour [INVENTED].**

- Watermelons from the Oxus.
- Felts stacked like roof tiles.
- A Watch crier repeating the blade ordinance from the gate board.
- Prices are high, because tomorrow is Friday and everything shuts.

**Shopping.** If the party needs to restock after the pass, it happens here. Shops close Friday, so tell them so.

## 8. The Keep and the Mercenaries (11174; barracks 11145 / 11151)

**The Keep is the seat of the Mingh household.** The data lists no adult head. The eldest Mingh present are **Bibigul** (19, non-binary, non-religious) and **Mehrdad** (18, the Lamp). **[INVENTED]** The head of the house is away, on campaign or at the Confederation's council. The real business is run by the advisor **Shokir Navidzoda** (59, the Fire; Social, Tier 2, Difficulty 13). Two young heirs and an old advisor make a quiet power vacuum in a city full of hired swords. Leave it on the table.

**Keep guards.**

- Doniyor Kipchak (50) and Ergash Karimzadeh (79). Both are Standard: Difficulty 12, HP 5, Stress 3, thresholds 6 / 11, +2, spear 1d8+1.
- Dilshad Sabirzoda, Saman Sarbazugli and Olim Chigil also stand guard.

**The mercenary companies.** They hold two barracks beside the Keep, 12 rank and file each. Their HQs (11148 and 11153) have no staff in the data. *"The Watch holds the street and the company holds the contract."*

- **Farid Wolf-Road** (Tulpar, 51). A recruiter who has noticed the wolf blood still on the party's gear **[INVENTED]**.
- **Iskander Split-Banner** (Tulpar, 88). An old banner-man and a source of stories.
- **Gulnoza Gokturk-Ash**, **Ramesh Ash-Horn**, **Manuchehr Boranian** (Gavar), and **Toqta Temurian the Younger** (Tulpar, 37).
- All are Standard: Difficulty 12, HP 5, Stress 3, thresholds 6 / 11, +2, spear 1d8+1.

**The job [INVENTED].** A grain convoy is going south toward **Puli Khumri** after market day. It is short on guards, because the companies' best are with the absent lord. The pay is fair, and the road is the one the party wants anyway. This is the cleanest hook out, and it lines up with Hilola's letter.

**Bathhouse rumours (any one) [INVENTED].**

- "The Mingh children sign whatever the old man puts in front of them."
- "A stone man asked the militia for the law. The *law*."
- "Chain-sign riders north of the pass. Not the Pork and Crow lot, the other ones."
- "Stars fell over the mountains last month." This ties to the Night of Falling Stars from the Session 3 prep.
