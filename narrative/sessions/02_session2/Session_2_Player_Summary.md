# Session 2 — Rabati Malik


Volkath's caravan reached Rabati Malik with trouble already on the road. Captain Idris Kaan and his soldiers were riding in to press the tariff dispute, and somebody had set a trap for them in the streets behind the market. The trap belonged to Temir, Volkath's son.

The party split. Ariel and Reizan flew out after Kaan. Rill and Cal went looking for the ambush. Sahir stayed at the gates.

- **Kaan** agreed to ride to the gates, but not through them. He wanted to see what he could see first.
- **Cal** found the ambush with Rill. Temir and at least one other young Tulpar threw things down at Cal from a roof and threatened him. The things hit him but did no real harm.
- **Rill** talked them down. Temir was scolded in front of his group and then taken back in. Sanem and the other Order of the Chain dissidents stayed uneasy about the plan after that.
- **Kaan** called the whole thing a misunderstanding.
- **Volkath** gave the party information, supplies, and his best wishes. The caravan moved on.
  
While traveling the roads, it became clear that the party, while headed roughly in the same direction as the wayward warrior, they would most likely not catch up. Ariel, with agreement from the remaining crew, flew forward to attempt one last conversation. Finding the warrior well at home on the road, but not looking for company, Ariel set forth her arguments. Apparently, that argument was sufficient to convince Veyra to wait for the group to arrive, and to continue to travel with them for a while.

On the next evening, a traveller came to the fire alone. He played down the fact that he was on the road by himself, and asked to share the fire. He gave very little useful information. He did show his wares to explain his trade: a coil of rope that never seemed to run out, and other goods, each with a trader's patter. He left in the night, against the group's warnings, with little fuss.
