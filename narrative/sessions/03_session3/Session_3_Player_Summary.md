# Session 3

## Chapter 4: Dire Pass & Legal Woes

The companions had bedded down for the night on a windswept mountain pass, still days from their destination, when the quiet of the watch was shattered by a pack of enormous black wolves closing in on the caravan's horses. What followed was a brief but ferocious battle fought in the dark, with chaos magic, arrows, and blades cutting down the beasts until the survivors fled howling into the night. The party emerged bloodied but victorious, and the next day's travel brought them down from the mountains and into a small agricultural town where they sought rest, drink, and, in [[cal|Cal]]'s case, a thorough reading of the local legal code. The session ended with the group settling into the town's tavern and archery range, enjoying a rare moment of levity before the road to [[Qarshi]] resumed at dawn.

### Key Events

1. **The Watch and the Wind**

The night had settled cold and gusty over the mountain camp, and the companions were arranging their rest with the wary practicality of travelers who knew the road's dangers. Ariel declared her intention to sleep in a tent, confident in her comfort, while [[Veyra]] dryly noted that her wings would be useless inside canvas and that the wind might simply carry the whole structure away. The talk turned to the order of watches, and [[Cal]] made it clear that he did not trust Veyra enough to let her stand watch alone, a suspicion she accepted with an indifference that bordered on amusement. The fire burned low, the horses shifted in their lines, and the first watch began with Cal listening to the mountain dark.

2. **Rustling Among the Horses**

Halfway through his watch, Cal heard movement near the horses, a disturbance too deliberate to be the wind, though he tried to convince himself otherwise. He stepped closer, senses straining, to find the animals spooked, eyes rolling, hooves stamping. That is when he saw them: dark shapes, roughly half the size of a horse, moving low and skulking toward the herd. He called out for everyone to wake, and the camp stirred to sudden, urgent life. The crew roused themselves, and the companions gathered their weapons as the dark shapes resolved into something far worse than scavengers.

3. **Chaos Unleashed**

Cal planted himself like a wall and pointed at the nearest shadowy figure, shouting the word of his power as sickly gray, opalescent energy arced from his finger. The bolt of chaos was not any single color but every color at once, and it struck the nearest creature, illuminating a massive, wolfish form with dark fur and burning eyes. The beast staggered under the magical assault, but its packmates surged forward, leaping and clawing. Cal took the brunt of their fury, his stone and wood frame absorbing blows that would have felled a lesser being, though he was forced to draw on his reserves to weather the onslaught.

4. Rill's Vantage

While the wolves pressed the attack, [[Rill]] scrambled up the cliff face with the agility of his simian heritage, seeking higher ground from which to strike. He reached a ledge above the fray and, without hesitation, threw himself from the mountainside at the wolves, driving his blade down through a wolf's neck and into its chest. The creature sputtered and died on his sword, and Rill stood wreathed in shadow, his patron's power flowing through him. Ariel, launching into the air, remarked with dark humor on the wolves fate, and Veyra offered a mock-sympathetic note for the poor thing before the battle demanded their full attention.

5. Ariel''s Aerial Barrage

[[Ariel]] climbing to a position alongside their cliff path, positioning herself beyond the reach of snapping jaws, and drew her bow with practiced calm. She loosed an arrow that caught one wolf mid-lunge, sending it tumbling backward, and then another shot aided by the powerful drumbeats from her wings that struck with such force that the creature was hurled from the cliff's edge. She called, and it was clear she had no intention of falling victim to the wolves' lunges, her wings beating steadily as she rained death from above. The wolves that tried to leap for her found only empty air.

6. Veyra Joins the Fray

Veyra sprinted across the rocky ground to close the distance, her longsword drawn and ready. She reached the melee and carved an upper slash across a wolf's throat, sending gouts of blood into the night air. The beast staggered back toward the cliff's edge, and she tried to kick it over, but it scrambled away, still alive and furious. She cursed the miss but pressed on, her blade flashing in the dim light as she fought alongside the others. The battle was chaos, but it was a chaos she understood, and she moved through it with the grim efficiency of someone who had done this many times before.

7. **The Wolves Break**

The pack's numbers dwindled rapidly under the combined assault. Rill pulled his sword free from the first kill and drove it into another wolf with lethal precision, while Cal channeled his chaos through his fists, a glowing purple shockwave coruscating in a colorful wave around and past an unsuspecting Veyra , and slamming into two beasts at once. Ariel continued to fire from above, and Veyra cut down another wolf with a clean strike. The remaining creatures, seeing their packmates fall and bodies tumbling from the cliff, lost their nerve. One wolf looked at the other, and without a sound, they turned and fled into the darkness, their howls receding down the mountain path. Ariel flew over the route to confirm there were no more coming, and the night fell quiet once more.

8. **Aftermath and Dawn**

The companions stood amid the carnage, breathing hard, the scent of dire wolf blood thick in the cold air. Cal expressed his distaste for the whole affair, and the group set about calming the horses and clearing the bodies from the camp. The crew, who had slept through most of the fight, woke in the morning to find the battle already won, and they regarded the travelers with a mixture of awe and confusion. The rest of the night passed uneventfully, with only distant howls echoing from far away, and the party took their long rest as the sun rose over the mountains.

9. **Descent from the Pass**

The morning brought clear skies and a steady descent from the high pass, the horses growing calmer as the altitude dropped and the terrain softened into rolling plains. The party saw more traffic on the road and in the fields, a sign that they were approaching civilization once more. Veyra noted the increased activity with a wary eye, remarking that wherever there were people, there was trouble. Cal, meanwhile, seemed preoccupied with thoughts of law and governance, and when a pair of riders approached wearing the colors of a local militia, he immediately began asking about legal codes.

10. **The Militia Riders**

The riders hailed the caravan and introduced themselves as town militia, warning that violence would be met with violence but that honest traders were welcome. Cal asked if they had a code of laws, and when the lead rider admitted she could not produce a law book, he pressed her on where one might be found. The militiawoman, clearly nonplussed by the strange stone figure's questions, said he would have to ask in town and rode off with her partner. Cal remarked afterward that he did not think they had laws at all, and Veyra agreed that it was probably a better form of government than most.

11. **The Town and the Longhouse**

The party arrived in the town by late afternoon, a medium-sized settlement with military buildings, a civil government hall, and a central road leading toward Qarshi. Cal immediately made for the longhouse to see the code of laws, with Rill accompanying him to translate his friend's bluntness into something approaching diplomacy. Inside, they encountered an elderly doorman who promised to fetch someone who could help, then disappeared. Cal grew impatient and began opening doors, which drew the attention of two militia members who insisted he wait. The situation grew increasingly absurd as the old man returned, then left again, and Rill explained to the militiamen that his friend had a talent for making people angry without meaning to. Cal was (eventually) granted a look at the local laws, written on several sheafs unfolded at a nearby reading table.

12. **The Archery Contest and the Tavern**

While Cal and Rill navigated the bureaucratic maze of the longhouse, Veyra and Ariel found the town tavern, a pleasant establishment with an archery range out back. A local man challenged them to a friendly contest, and Ariel proceeded to demonstrate her skill with a bow, hitting the bullseye despite the warped house bow. Veyra also took a shot, her crossbow experience translating well enough to graze the target. The challenger, clearly outmatched, excused himself to fetch drinks and never returned. Ariel and Veyra shared a knowing look and continued to enjoy the evening, the warmth of the tavern a welcome contrast to the cold mountain night before.

### Critical Decisions & Unresolved Threads

The party chose to stand and fight the dire wolves rather than attempt to flee, a decision that secured the camp's safety and sent a clear message to any other predators in the area.Cal's insistence on reading the town's legal code led to a strange and unresolved encounter with the local militia, who seemed both confused and suspicious of his request. The group also decided to spend the night in the town rather than push on toward [[Qarshi]], a choice that Veyra questioned but ultimately accepted. The strange behavior of Rill's coin, which twitched in his pocket during the celebration at the tavern, remains unexplained, as does the lingering sense among the companions that something about the town is not quite right.

### Next Session Hooks

The companions resumed their journey toward [[Qarshi]] at dawn. Tension with the militia and the strange atmosphere of the town lingered after the events of the night. Cal acquired knowledge of the local laws. The twitching of Rill's coin during the celebration at the tavern remained unexplained, as did the companions' shared sense that something about the town was not quite right.
