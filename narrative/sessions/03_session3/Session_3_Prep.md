## 2. Options

The option sets are designed to combine. Recommended build: **B + C + D**, with an interrupt slot still to be chosen (see A).

### A — Interrupt (GM to choose one type)

The "mass" from Ariel's precipice memory is not reused as a fight, and the word "mass" is not used for any Session 3 threat. Three types, all **[INVENTED]**:

- **An animal threat: pack, flock, or swarm.** A plausible animal group: wolves in the high passes, a flock over a water point, a swarm at the fields. Real-world or magical analog, shown through practical effect.
- **A human group.** Regional pirates, or another armed band on the corridor. It can be negotiated, bought off, or fought.
- **An in-game environment event.** It may threaten or it may be purely atmosphere. It is not a combatant.

Whichever is chosen stays an interrupt, not the main event.

### B — Slow Crossing, Rabati Malik → Qarshi (Daggerheart environment)

No clock. No progress die, no fuse. The crossing is run as a Daggerheart environment: the GM works from a menu of events and conditions and checks each one against the scene as the party moves. Nothing forces a result, and the party sets the pace.

- **Conditions to check** **[INVENTED]** (at each waypoint, not on a timer):
  - Cold after dark. Frost on the high passes; felt and lamp oil matter.
  - Water at the next stop: the Sardoba cistern or the Nuratau spurs. **[UNVERIFIED]** Confirm which is on the true route before using either.
  - Supplies and mule condition.
- **Events to draw from** **[INVENTED]** (pick what fits the scene, or none):
  - A levy checkpoint on the road.
  - The Tulpar westbound levy column, passing the other way.
  - The eastbound Iron Bell convoy (see C).
  - A stranded traveler or a broken cart.
  - A rockfall on a pass path.
  - A Dwarven karez inspector checking water rights.
- Each event has local stakes only. No event is mandatory, and none is a countdown.

### C — Local Political Flavor

Use the register and the regional factions to make the road feel governed. Nothing here needs a roll.

- Imperial levy notices at the next posting, with the Rabati Malik tariff dispute still open. The register gives a seven-day documentation period. Use it as a deadline the party can see.
- A Tulpar westbound levy is pending. The party can see the pressure without being asked to solve it.
- An eastbound Iron Bell Company convoy to Khotan, three days out. The party can ride with them, trade with them, or avoid them.
- Waycross Junction is still reporting disturbances. No Dwarven official account exists. The register gives three explanations; the party can hear all three from different people.

### D — Timing: Mid-to-Late Autumn

- High passes beginning to hold frost; cold nights on the crossing.
- Lamps and felt matter more; the caravan has a reason to buy them.
- Use this as the reason the cold condition in B is live, not as a separate threat.

## 5. Burned City

**Kitabdara** ("valley of books"). **[INVENTED]**

- **Name check:** no existing use found in the repo. Searched for "Kitabdara" across `world/`, `narrative/`, `characters/`, `templates/`, and `mechanics/`, excluding archive. No hits.
- **Placement:** a few days' ride from Ember Wash and Kattakurgan. Off the main route, so it is a choice.
- **History:** the Azure Convocation kept an archive there. It was burned in the Scholar's Purge (~1175 HB equivalent). Locals say the fire came from the river side. Nobody will say who lit it.
- **Hook:** the burning is the same event that eliminated scholars close to the Wizard's plan. Traces of the archive may survive in the ash.
- **Connection to Ember Wash:** Ember Wash is the Warrior's awakening site (`world/locations/ember-wash.md`). Kitabdara is the nearest burned city. Not a required stop.

## 7. Nādir Qissakhān and "The Roads"

**The Roads** (`world/lore/The Roads.md`, public, status canon) is a lore piece whose storyteller portrait is captioned: "Chronicles of Empires and Dust: Nādir Qissakhān — from the Azure Convocation, 1175th year of 'Held Breath'."

**The Roads text.** The line "A thousand years, give or take, since the last empire crumbled" now reads "Since the last great empire crumbled." It carries no number, so it no longer conflicts with the regional doc's 150–200 years. (See conflict 1.)

**From the gap-fill (GM-confirmed):**
- Nādir is living.
- Azure Convocation scholar.
- Now in an educational role near or in Puli Khumri.
- Samarkand, and perhaps further east, appear in his history.

**Proposals:**
- **Author of The Roads:** Nādir, written in the period of his teaching. **[INVENTED]** Date of writing not stated in the piece. Propose 1190s HB equivalent, after the Purge.
- **Why he wrote it:** to record the roads before the Convocation's knowledge is lost. **[INVENTED]**
- **What he knows of the Night:** the Night is a folk memory in the Convocation archive. He can describe the falling stars and the Wizard's undead movements under cover of the omen, but not the Wizard's plan. **[INVENTED]**
- **Puli Khumri placement:** the Azure outpost there is invented. **[INVENTED]** Confirm before the party finds it.
