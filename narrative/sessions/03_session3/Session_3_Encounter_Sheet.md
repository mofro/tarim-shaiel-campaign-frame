## Part 1 — Environment: The Slow Crossing

Rabati Malik to Qarshi. No clock, no progress die, no fuse. Nothing forces a result, and the party sets the pace.

### Card

**The Slow Crossing**
*Tier 1 · Traversal*

*Frost on the high passes, and water measured in days.* **[INVENTED]**

**Impulses:** ration the water, keep the mules moving, find shelter before dark **[INVENTED]**

**Difficulty:** Special. Set per check (see Conditions).

**Potential Adversaries:** Any, only if an event from the menu calls for one. The crossing has no adversary of its own.

**Passive: Cold After Dark** **[INVENTED mechanic]**
Once the sun is down, the party needs felt or lamp oil to rest warm. Without it, each rest costs a Stress.
*GM question: who in the party has a cloak that will hold, and who is counting on the caravan's felt?*

**Passive: Water to the Next Stop** **[INVENTED mechanic]**
At each waypoint, check whether the water lasts to the next stop. The candidates are the Sardoba cistern and the Nuratau spurs. **[UNVERIFIED]** Confirm which one lies on the true route before naming either in play.
*GM question: who knows how far the next water is, and how sure are they?*

**Action: Tend the Mules** **[INVENTED mechanic]**
A character spends a turn on the animals. On success, the supplies hold for the stretch. On failure, one mule goes lame and slows the caravan.
*GM question: who is the caravan's keeper for this stretch?*

**Reaction: Waypoint Draw** **[INVENTED mechanic]**
When the party reaches a waypoint, draw one event from the menu below, or draw none.
*GM question: does this stop need an event, or does the road stay quiet?*

No countdown, by design (`Session_3_Prep.md` §2B). No Fear costs are set on these features.

### Conditions Checked at Waypoints

Checked at each waypoint, not on a timer (`Session_3_Prep.md` §2B).

| Condition | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cold after dark | Felt or lamp oil | Frost on the high passes. Mid-to-late autumn (§2D). |
| Water to the next stop | Water against the distance to the next stop | Source **[UNVERIFIED]** (see Passive above). |
| Supplies and mule condition | Tend the Mules (Action) | The caravan's felt, rope, and shoes matter here. |

### Event Menu

Pick one per waypoint, or none. Each event has local stakes only, and none is mandatory.

| Event | Who is there | Question for the scene |
|---|---|---|
| Levy checkpoint on the road | Imperial Road Authority | Does the party pay, argue, or pass? The register's seven-day documentation period is visible here. |
| Tulpar westbound levy column | Southern Tulpar Confederacy | The column is going the other way. Does it stop? |
| Eastbound Iron Bell convoy | Iron Bell Company, three days out to Khotan (§2C) | Ride with them, trade with them, or avoid them? |
| Stranded traveler or broken cart | Whoever is on the road | Do they need help, and what do they say? |
| Rockfall on a pass path | The road itself | Does the path close, and for how long? |
| Dwarven karez inspector | Dwarven Mountain Confederation | Checking water rights. Who holds the right to this water? |

## Part 2 — Wolf Interrupt

An interrupt, not the main event (`Session_3_Prep.md` §2A: "wolves in the high passes"). Prep §2B puts the crossing in frost on the high passes, so the wolves belong there. This card covers the animal interrupt. The human-group and environment-event options from §2A are not carded here.

### Stat Card

**Dire Wolf**
*Tier 1 Skulk*
*Lean grey shapes on the high passes, keeping pace with the caravan.* **[INVENTED]**

**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 5 / 9 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +2 | **Claws** | Melee | 1d6+2 phy

**Motives & Tactics:** Defend territory, harry, protect pack, surround, trail. The SRD gives motives only.

**FEATURES**
*Pack Tactics*. Type UNVERIFIED. **[INVENTED]** text: When another wolf in the pack is within Close range of the target, this wolf's attacks against it gain +1.
*Hobbling Strike*. Type UNVERIFIED. **[INVENTED]** text: On a hit, the target's movement is limited to Very Close until the end of its next turn.

### Notes

- **Source.** The stat line, thresholds, HP, stress, ATK, weapon, damage, and motives are the SRD Dire Wolf as published. The SRD gives feature names only.
- **Pack size.** One card per wolf. Two for a party of four, as the working default. **[INVENTED]** Reasons: each Dire Wolf has 4 HP, so two can each go down in a turn or two; two wolves are enough to trigger a pack feature; and the interrupt should not take over the session. Budget check, not done: a community report puts a four-player budget at 14 battle points and a Solo at 5 (unverified). No battle-point cost for the Dire Wolf was found, so the count cannot be checked against that budget yet. The PC sheets have no HP or Stress, and every sheet lists Evasion 10, which may be a placeholder. The party's real durability is unknown.
- **Features.** Pack Tactics and Hobbling Strike keep their SRD names. Their text is a draft. **[INVENTED]** A fan wiki gives different text. Its Pack Tactics adds damage (1d6+5) and a Fear when another wolf is in melee range of the target. Its Hobbling Strike deals 3d4+10, which looks too high next to 1d6+2 claws. Neither fan text is adopted. If the fan Pack Tactics is confirmed, pack size becomes the main power lever: a single wolf gets no bonus, and a pair hits harder.
- **Verify before play.** `Session_3_Prep.md` §12 asks for the stat block to be verified if the interrupt is a group. The SRD cannot settle the feature text; the core rulebook can.
