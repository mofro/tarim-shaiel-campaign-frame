The lamp on the clerk's table had burned the same oil for eleven years. I know this because he told me, unprompted, the way men confess small continuities when larger ones have failed them. When I asked what he recorded, he named categories: arrivals, departures, quantities, origins declared at the gate. When I asked what he did not record, he was quiet for longer than the question seemed to warrant.

A tool that answers truly is not the same as a tool that answers completely. The merchants of this stretch learned that early, in the matter of scales — a scale tells you what a thing weighs, not what a thing is worth, and the gap between those two facts has funded several dynasties.

What the lamp remembered, and what the clerk wrote down, were not always the same.

— Attributed to Yūsra al-Mahdi, itinerant chronicler / recovered from a caravanserai wall, 1159 HB
