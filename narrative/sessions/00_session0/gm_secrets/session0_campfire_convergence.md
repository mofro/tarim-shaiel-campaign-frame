# Session 0 Campfire — Convergence & Recognition

The four heroes met at the campfire outside Waycross. Each arrived carrying their awakening wound; none yet understood what the others had experienced. The convergence was the first moment they were in the same place with the same sky overhead.

Volkath told his story — a compressed account of the last decade of history in the region: the collapse of the old imperial roads, the current factional pressures, the reason caravans like his move by night now. This history is the players' first grounded context for the world they've woken into.

## Source documents for Volkath's account

- **Prose narrative:** `narrative/sessions/01_session1/world_primer.md` — the emotional and contextual version; how it felt to live through it
- **Dry history:** `narrative/newspaper/0002-session-0-convergence/08-orders-of-the-chain.md` — the factual record; what happened and when

## Recognition note

The campfire is also where the players first recognized each other as something more than fellow travelers — the recognition is implicit and incomplete at this stage. None of them name it. The scene ends before anything is resolved. The full Three-Layer Revelation structure begins from here.
