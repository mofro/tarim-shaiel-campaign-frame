# Session 1 — Opening Run Sheet

⚠️ **GM-ONLY. Do not share with players.**

**Players:** Lisa (Warrior), Erik (Seeker), Arno (Sentinel), Marc (Visionary/Seraph) — plus **Jim** (Bard, Rahban) arriving this session, and **Dave** (Warlock, Vanara — patron confirmed: Door One, Lord of Exhausted Seams) seeded this session.

Picks up exactly where Session 0 cut: Volkath, mid-word, at the campfire.

---

## Beat 1 — Cold Open: Jim at the Fork

- Run `narrative/sessions/00_session0/Session_0_Jim_Introduction.md` as written — it's complete, don't improvise around it.
- Reminders:
  - Both roads are genuinely valid. Don't steer.
  - Merchant (Jade Illusionist) never explains "have you considered both might be yours?" — deflect if pressed, per file.
  - Either path ends the same: Jim reaches the caravan fire "later than he would have," sits down because it's warm and he's Rahban.
- He lands at the fire **after** Beat 2 below — he missed the vision. Don't let him ask about it. Don't over-explain.

---

## Beat 2 — The Vision (read verbatim, don't improvise)

- Let the fire be ordinary a few minutes first — people eating, settling. Don't rush to this.
- The Seraph (Marc) triggers it: the obsidian disk catches firelight, he looks up.
- **Signal:** pause the table, wait for quiet, then read:

> Looking up from your shared bowls around the caravan campfire, your eyes lock. Electric. You all have a moment of shared realization.
>
> The vision becomes clear — you all remember a different moment, sitting together. Strong, artful architecture that speaks of care, longevity, and peace. Voices of others, greeting you as peers and familiars. All of you powerful and confident. Serene and somehow... content. It feels like... home.
>
> The vision lingers for a heartbeat. Then it fades.

- Silence after. Don't ask "so what do you do." Let it sit.
- If asked what it meant: **"That's what we're going to find out."** Full stop.

---

## Beat 3 — Volkath's Slip, Then "Listen—"

- New this session: his composure slips half a beat *before* he speaks — he felt something too, doesn't know what. A flicker, not a scene of its own.
- He speaks into the silence: **"Listen—"** — and is cut off mid-word by Jim's arrival. Mundane, oblivious, right on top of it.
- Character beat: how do the four react to each other in that second, in front of a man who just proved he pays closer attention than "merchant" accounts for? Don't script it — let the table fill it.
