# The Jade Coast Realms

> ⚠️ **Stub — basic pass only. Status: draft — maritime power at campaign's edges, not yet in active play zone. Elven whisper-presence at court is established (see _category.md note and DM Notes); details TBD.**

## Overview

Dynastic maritime states controlling the southern routes at the eastern terminus of the Silk Road. Cultural and scholarly prestige complements maritime power — the Realms' scholarly institutions hold knowledge that is not available through the overland routes. Their sustained tension with the Eastern Imperial Dominion is the defining eastern conflict, and the Elven Highland Enclaves have been quietly managing both sides for long enough that neither remembers a time without Elven presence at their courts.
