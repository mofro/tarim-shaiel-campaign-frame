# Dwarven Tarim Authority

> ⚠️ **Stub — basic pass only.**

## Overview

The actual power behind the Tarim Basin's governance — an open secret among those who know how to look. The Dwarven Tarim Authority controls the karez water systems that make Tarim Basin civilization possible, and therefore controls everything that depends on that water. The Human Tarim Councils are the visible administrative face; the Authority is the structural reality underneath. This is not hidden because it is shameful — it is simply how the arrangement works, and changing it would require someone to be willing to let the water stop.
