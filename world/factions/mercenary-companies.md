# The Mercenary Companies

> ⚠️ **Stub — working name and basic pass only.** Written for the FTG town layer (#406) so Qarshi's two Mercenary Company headquarters have a faction to seat. The name, the companies and their employers are placeholders for the GM to rename or replace.

## Overview

Hired swords quartered in the towns of the Central Asian hubs, paid by a caravan house, a council or whoever holds the Keep that season. They are not the town watch: they answer to their company captain and to the contract, and a town tolerates them while the coin holds.

## Structure & Governance

Each headquarters is a seat, with the barracks beside it housing its rank and file. A company is led by a captain who signs the contracts; the companies compete for the same work and do not share an oath.

## Relationship to Power

**Visible face:** armed men in company colours around the Keep and the caravan quarter.

**Relation to the Oxus Watch Order:** the Watch keeps the streets and gates for the town; the companies are hired for the roads, the caravans and the Keep's own errands. Where the two meet, the Watch holds the street and the company holds the contract.
