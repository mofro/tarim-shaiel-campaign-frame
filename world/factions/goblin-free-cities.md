# The Free Cities (Goblin)

> ⚠️ **Stub — basic pass only. Supersedes former `goblin-trade-networks` entry.**

## Overview

Goblin-governed city-states distributed across urban nodes along all major routes. Not a political confederation — they do not answer to each other — but they share civic norms, mutual recognition of Free City status, and the practical understanding that their value lies in being genuinely neutral ground. Markets operate under Free City rules; disputes adjudicate under Free City procedures; travelers of any faction can do business without that business being reported to whoever they are avoiding.
