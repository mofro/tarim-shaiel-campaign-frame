# The Oxus Watch Order

> ⚠️ **Stub — working name and basic pass only.** Written for the FTG town layer (#387). The name, founding and chain of command are placeholders for the GM to rename or replace.

## Overview

The standing watch of the Central Asian towns: the guardsmen who man a town's barracks, keep its gates and answer to a local captain rather than to a confederation's warlord. A town's watch is a local body; the Order is what its members swear into, so a guardsman in Dehdadi and one in a neighbouring town share an oath, a badge and a name for the work.

## Structure & Governance

Each town's barracks is a seat. A captain commands the seat; the Order itself has no ruler, only a custom of captains meeting when a road or river crossing is in dispute. Seats are fed and paid by their town, which is where the Order's independence ends.

## Relationship to Power

**Visible face:** the town watch. It is seen at every gate and barracks, and it answers to the town first.

**Relation to the Tulpar Confederation (Samarkand):** the Confederation is the regional power of the Balkh country; the Order keeps order inside the towns the Confederation does not garrison itself. Where they overlap, the watch defers on matters of war and the Confederation defers on matters of the street.
