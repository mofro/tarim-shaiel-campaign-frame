# Merchant Guilds (Multiethnic)

> ⚠️ **Stub — basic pass only.**

## Overview

Not a single organization but a category: multiethnic trading guilds with shared professional norms and mutual recognition protocols operating across all regions. Their structural neutrality — they need all factions as customers, so they cannot afford permanent enemies — makes them the Silk Road's connective tissue. They know more about what moves across the routes than any single political power, because information is cargo and cargo is their business. Their neutrality is mercantile, not principled.
