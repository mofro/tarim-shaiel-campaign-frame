## Locations

Master registry of all named locations in Tarim-Shaiel. Each row represents a linkable entity. Column values are the canonical strings for use in frontmatter `factions:` fields across location and event files.

**Status key:** `canon` — established lore | `draft` — in development | `color` — world texture, not active in play zone | `proposed` — speculative, needs approval | `tbd` — placeholder, needs definition
