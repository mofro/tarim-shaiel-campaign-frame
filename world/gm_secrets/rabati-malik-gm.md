# Rabati Malik — GM Planning & Environment

Planning reference and reusable scene mechanics, not narrative content. Transcluded into the public location page at `world/locations/rabati-malik.md`.

Rabati Malik has hosted two major beats so far: Ariel's Session 0 courtyard standoff (the tariff dispute with the imperial patrol), and the Session 2 crisis in its streets (Volkath's son's ambush of that same patrol — see `narrative/sessions/02_session2/gm_secrets/Session_2_GM_NOTES.md`). Expect it to keep recurring as a caravan waypoint.

---

## Environment Stat Block

**RABATI MALIK**
Tier 1 · Social
A fortified caravanserai where two authorities — the Merchant Council's tariff agreements and the empire's fading claim to collect — never quite resolve who's actually in charge.

**Impulses:** keep the peace long enough to trade, extract what the moment allows, look busy when it's safer than looking

**Difficulty:** 11
**Potential Adversaries:** Imperial Patrol, Merchant, Chain-Breakers Order sympathizers

**FEATURES**

*Two Ledgers — Passive:* Merchants here keep genuine books and a second set for imperial inspection. A PC who understands trade, or takes a moment to build rapport, can get real information the second ledger would otherwise hide.

*What's the Talk of the Caravanserai — Passive:* A PC can ask merchants, staff, or patrons about local events, rumors, and potential work with a Presence Roll. On a success, pick two details below — three on a critical success. On a failure, pick one and mark a Stress as the local carries on about something irrelevant.
- A fascinating rumor with a connection to a PC's background
- A promising job involving a nearby threat or situation
- Local folklore relating to something they've seen
- Town gossip hinting at a community problem

*Look Busy — Reaction:* When imperial livery enters the courtyard, activate — describe how the whole courtyard's tempo shifts without anyone saying why.

---

## GM Notes

The tariff friction here isn't cosmetic — it's the standing condition that produced both major scenes so far. Any future visit can pull on it again without needing a new justification.

Captain Idris Kaan's patrol operates out of or through here. Volkath's caravan has a working, if uneasy, relationship with the place — reflected in "Two Ledgers."
