## Wit and Wisdom

Tarim-Shaiel has many voices. Some are foolish, some are wise. Some echo across vast distances and some are whispered dark places. Many only exist to please themselves. All are remembered in little ways and large by the people who live, fight, play, and die in this world.

For those words that manage to be collected and kept, this is but a paltry collection.

> **Note for Players:** This section collects not only the sage records of scholars and regents, but also the lowly and clever. Your characters may find themselves so enshrined for pith and wisdom, and that is encouraged and encouraging! Always be willing to tell your own tale...
