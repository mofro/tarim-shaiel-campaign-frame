# Cosmology

## The Mythic Ecosystem 🔒
- Warrens = distinct dimensional spaces (worlds/planes), not metaphors for power flow
- Functions as a **biome**: rule-governed, not designed or aware
- "Power draws power" — concentrated power attracts more pressure; bends ecosystem toward it
- Affinities are not interchangeable; attuned creatures/people cannot swap allegiances
- Players encounter it only through practical effects — no exhaustive cosmological exposition intended

## The Held Breath 🔒
- The cosmic threat = **multiple liminal consciousnesses at celestial scale**
- NOT a single sleeping entity, NOT a geological/tectonic force — use "Held Breath" only
- Not imprisoned — held *back* by cosmological equilibrium
- Not actively malevolent — not awake enough to have mortal-recognizable intent
- True danger: human meddling (Wizard's necromantic assault at scale) risks tipping scales enough to begin waking them
- Horror = their *weight*, not malice

## Liberation Framing 🔒
- Liberation = **massive beacon event** — "power draws power" expressing at scale
- Shattering thousands of binding spells simultaneously rang through Warrens like a bell
- Consequences = **Warren disturbance + attention from entities with agendas** — NOT ecosystem damage
- 1,000-year gap: those agendas have been running since the heroes entered Hero Heaven
- Do NOT use "ecosystem damage" framing — use "Warren disturbance" / "interested parties"

## R/H/K System 🔒 (reframed 2026-03-08)
- Tools are **Warren ambassadors** — their R/H/K reflects Warren interests, not loyalty to the hero
- **Resist** — Warren protecting its investment, OR protecting itself from what hero is about to do
- **Hunger** — a Warren (or denizens) calling for energy use, for reasons hero may not understand
- **Know** — Warren-mediated revelation: what the Warren *wants* hero to know (may be true, partial, or strategically framed)
- NOT the tool being protective of the hero; NOT neutral truth-delivery

## The Wizard 🔒 (locked 2026-03-17)
- **Option B + C combined**: tragic hubris seeded by cosmic manipulation
- Knows about ecosystem risks and the Held Breath — acts in *overconfidence*, not ignorance
- The Held Breath entity has shaped his certainty over 1,000 years — his conviction is not purely his own
- Plans will appear *almost* coherent to players who piece them together — more unsettling than cartoonish evil
- Motivation for *why* he wants to breach the Threshold: separate writing task, not yet resolved ❓

## Three-Layer Revelation 🔒
- Layer 1 (early): Stop the Wizard — surface quest
- Layer 2 (mid-late): Wizard's Threshold breach causes immediate cosmological consequence — Held Breath stirs
- Layer 3 (endgame): Stopping the Wizard is necessary but not sufficient — heroes must reckon with Warren-level agendas disturbed by the liberation 1,000 years ago

## Threshold 🔒
- Hero Heaven is adjacent to Warren structure — wired directly into the infrastructure
- Breaching it = **immediate, unmistakable consequence** (kicks a load-bearing wall, not slow accumulation)
- The Wizard's assault causes something to break *right now*, visibly
