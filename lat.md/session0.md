# Session 0

## Core Architecture 🔒
- Heroes wake individually, solve crisis, converge on westward caravan, campfire recognition ends the session
- Three-layer narrative: Hero Heaven → Legendary Past → Current World (heroes remember nothing)
- Heroes NOT punished — earned paradise, recalled for reasons unknown to them (possibly cosmic conscription — see ❓ in [[lat.md/cosmology]])
- Dual-truth preserved: GM knows cosmological facts; players experience only emotional/sensory truth

## 7-Segment Pacing 🔒
- ~145 minutes actual play; 4.5–5 hours with natural pacing
- Seg 0: Opening framework (10 min)
- Seg 1: Warrior + Sacrificer parallel awakenings (25 min)
- Seg 2: Bridge + Seeker parallel awakenings (25 min)
- Seg 3: Breaker solo/sequential (30–35 min, high-tension)
- Seg 4: Visionary crisis pulling toward convergence (20 min)
- Seg 5: Convergence & caravan boarding (20 min)
- Seg 6: Campfire recognition climax — GM-imposed narration, cut to black (10 min)
- Seg 7: Closing & notes (5 min)

## Awakening Locations (per archetype) 🔒
- **Warrior** — Refugee camp (~30 people in ravine); protect through restraint, not dominance
- **Bridge** — Prison cell; negotiate freedom when compromise seems impossible
- **Seeker** — Desert wasteland; stars don't match charts; knowledge vs. instinct to find water
- **Sacrificer** — Poor family's home (being cared for); accept being the recipient of sacrifice
- **Breaker** — Collapsed inn after earthquake; abandon hammer (it's structural support) or refuse and find another way
- **Visionary** — Mid-crisis (avalanche/fire/bandits NOW); act with no time for foresight

## Secret Snippet Architecture 🔒
- Delivered privately at narrative climax of each awakening's crisis
- Sensory + emotional register only — NOT explanatory
- Player reads silently; other players don't see unless recipient shares
- Purpose: player-to-character investment without revealing cosmic truth
- 30–60 seconds reading time; earned through the crisis, not gift-wrapped

## 5 Shared Memory Events 🔒
- **Event 1: The Warrior's Choice** — Method of engagement (restraint/aggression/diplomacy); pairings: Warrior+Bridge, Warrior+Breaker, Seeker+Warrior
- **Event 2: The Question of Sight** — What we know vs. trust vs. what's actually true; pairings: Seeker+Bridge, Visionary+Bridge, Seeker+Visionary
- **Event 3: The Weight of Choice** — Action vs. inaction, cost vs. purpose; pairings: Breaker+Sacrificer (opposed), Breaker+Sacrificer (shared), Visionary+Sacrificer
- **Event 4: Aspirations Undone** — Gap between what they fought for and what emerged; any characters discovering unintended consequences
- **Event 5: The Wizard's Shadow** — What they suspect about the Wizard's goals (and why they're wrong); plants false certainty
- SME distribution: 1–2 at CP1, 3–4 at CP3, 5 at CP5

## 4-Axis Flashback Trigger System 🔒
1. **Solo — power progression**: mechanical thresholds tied to advancement
2. **Solo — environmental/event**: returning to significant locations, moral echoes
3. **Solo — personal crisis**: archetype's core doubt mirrored in present situation
4. **Interaction**: 2+ heroes working in concert unlock shared memories (event-centric design avoids combinatorial explosion)

## Recognition Climax (GM-imposed) 🔒
- Campfire; all heroes lock eyes; GM narrates shared Hero Heaven vision
- Vision: emotionally specific, visually vague — architecture of care, voices as peers, serene
- Cut to black before anyone speaks; session ends on realization, not explanation
