## R/H/K (Warren-Mediated) 🔒
- Resist / Hunger / Know = Warren allegiance system, NOT tool loyalty or neutral truth
- Full detail: [[lat.md/cosmology]] § R/H/K System

## Tool 4-Stage Evolution 🔒
- Stage 0 (Session 0): Crude — functional, unremarkable, no magical properties
- Stage 1: Masterwork — refined, recognisable, minor magical resonance
- Stage 2: Ornate — clearly legendary, significant magical properties active
- Stage 3: Legendary — mythic artifact, full Warren ambassador role expressed
- [[mechanics/character-progression/TOOL_EVOLUTION_FRAMEWORK.md]] — full framework

## The Wrongness — Three-Tier Escalation 🔒
- Tier 1 (CP1–CP2): Localized dead zones — Warren disturbance as absence
- Tier 2 (CP3–CP4): Alive-wrong dead zones — something present, wrong in nature
- Tier 3 (CP5–CP6): Location endings — places stop being what they were
- Always framed as Warren disturbance; never as ecosystem damage

## Convergence Points (CP1–CP6) 🔒
- Not quests or plot waypoints — inevitable collisions between independently-moving world forces
- CP1 "The Chain-Breakers Know" (Sess 3–6) | CP2 "The Dwarven Archive Speaks" (Sess 7–11)
- CP3 "The Orcish Songs Are Literal" (Sess 10–14) | CP4 "The Mirror Cracks" (Sess 13–18)
- CP5 "The Wizard Is Already There" (Sess 17–22) | CP6 "The Companion Speaks Clearly" (Sess 21–26)
- Full: [[narrative/gm_secrets/MID_CAMPAIGN_CONVERGENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md]]
