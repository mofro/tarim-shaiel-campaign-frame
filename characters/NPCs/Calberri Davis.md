# Calberri Davis

⚠️ **GM-ONLY.** The party does not know who he is. Do not link this page from anything player-facing.

---

## Who He Is

An avatar of [[narrative/gm_secrets/divine-players/the-jade-illusionist|The Jade Illusionist]]. "Calberri Davis" is the name he gave in play, and it is one of the Jade Illusionist's listed aliases (GM, 2026-11-02).

He is a distinct persona from **Kalas**, the junction merchant of Session 1 (jade abacus, pays in kind). Each incarnation has its own persona, so nothing Kalas knew or did carries over to him.

---

## Session 2 Appearance

The evening after Rabati Malik, about a day out on the road, shortly before the party left it for the overland route to the southwest.

- **Arrival:** Came to the fire alone and played down travelling alone. Asked to share the fire.
- **Information:** Gave almost nothing useful.
- **Trade:** Explained what took him down the road by showing his wares. Magical items, impressive but, as far as anyone could tell, useless. One was a coil of rope that never seems to run out. Each came with a trader's patter. The other items were not recalled.
- **What he left:** A coin. Flipped or tossed, it hangs in the air and spins along the axis of the throw without slowing, and it can be plucked out of the spin with no difficulty or resistance. It was found in Rill's pocket after he had gone (GM, 2026-11-02). The hovering item and the gift are the same coin.
- **Departure:** Left in the night with little fuss, though everyone had cautioned him against going.

---

## Handling

Taken from the Jade Illusionist profile, so he plays the same way as every other incarnation:

- He never directly lies. What he says is true, what he omits is extensive, and any implication is chosen with care.
- Goods with no obvious provenance are his signature. The disguise should not be obvious. Recognition afterwards should give the players the sense that something important was already said.
- He leaves without warning when a scene turns dull. A sudden absence is not a defeat condition.
- He answers questions obliquely and partially. Certainty, instruction and plans are what he does not give.
