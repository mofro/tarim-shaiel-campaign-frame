### The Premise

sakldjlsakjd

**Tarim Shaiel** is a dark fantasy campaign set in a world that carries the weight of its past like a wound that never quite closed. It is a world with opinions: about consequence, about history, about the people who shaped it and the debts that haven't been settled. This is not a campaign about heroism. It is a campaign about what heroism actually costs. And who pays. (see the [Campaign Frame](Welcome%20to%20Tarim%20Shaiel/Campaign%20Frame.md))

### The Premise (Secret)

Heroes of mythic stature — comparable to Achilles, Hercules, the great champions of legend — who earned paradise through deeds that shook the world. Expelled not in shame, but because something they did, or failed to do, left a wound in the world that only they can close.

Built on [**Daggerheart**](https://www.example.com) [](https://www.example.com)by [Darrington Press](https://www.example.com) — a narrative-first TTRPG using a Hope/Fear dice duality. In our "home-brew", heroes must find their footing, seek out mythic influence and artifacts, and discover not only who they are, but what they can become...
